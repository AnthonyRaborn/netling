NETLING 2.0 SPRITE PROTOTYPE

Development only; spoilers. One Iron line, baby to elder (four forms), built two ways: model A composes the teen and the adult from bodies and overlays, model B authors them in full. Wearables are the real 1.0 ones. See docs/NETLING_2_SPRITES.md. Serve with npm run serve and open /prototype/netling2/.

THE LINE, MODEL A: COMPOSED

Baby, street-leaning teen, Gronk (Breach, street lean), elder. The teen is a body plus an overlay; Gronk is the adult body plus a role overlay plus a lean overlay. Baby and elder are the same art as in model B.

THE LINE, MODEL B: AUTHORED

THE THREE TEENS, AND THE HIDDEN BRANCH (authored)

The two main teens (corp, street) differ only slightly. The hidden-path teen has its own outline: taller, crowned, a third eye, a narrow neck. Second row: the hidden branch, baby, hidden-path teen, Guru. Guru's elder is not drawn.

ONE ELDER PER ADULT (authored)

Iron's nine adults on the first line and each one's elder below: the adult grown wider (18 columns against 16) and no taller than 15 rows, keeping its own marks. Splat, Gronk, Jiff, Bamf, Ping, Feep, Munch, Thrash, Guru. Names are not decided.

PROGRAM: BABY, TEENS, ADULTS AND ELDERS (authored from 1.0's art)

The Program egg. First row: baby (1.0's Bitling), the corp teen (1.0's Kernel), the street teen (new), the hidden teen (1.0's Shell). Second row: the nine adults, corp then street within a role (Tiger and Worm, Mouse and Spoof, Parse and Phreak, Gobble and Snarf), then Ghost (1.0's). Third row: each adult's elder, the adult stretched to 18 columns and one row taller with a mark of its own. Names of the teens and elders are not decided. The controls above apply (pose, wearable, anchors, neglect, bugs; the tell skin can be set to program).

WETWARE: BABY, TEENS, ADULTS AND ELDERS (first drafts)

The Wetware egg. First row: the organoid baby and the three teens (corp, street, hidden). Second row: the nine adults, corp then street within a role (Razor and Solo, Wired and Chipped, Mentat and Gibson, Nutri and Leech), then Blank. Third row: each adult's elder (Wired's is 1.0's Plat). Blank's elder is in motion: a camouflage band sweeps the hood and body and a ghost dub slides behind it (a 4.8 s loop; reduced motion parks it). That layer is the one place a form's outline changes on purpose; its registered frames still obey the frame rules. The controls above apply.

HOW MODEL A IS BUILT

Body (grey), role overlay (pink), lean overlay (yellow; erased cells dark red) and the result, in the A frame.

NEGLECT FROM UNMET NEEDS (Iron: rust)

Levels 0, 1 and 2 on each form. Driven by the four care sliders above (1.0's alert lines: Charge or Sync under 20, Heat over 80; earlier soft lines), transient, so it clears when the needs are met. It never moves and never changes the outline.

BUGS AS GLITCHES

0 to 5 bugs on Gronk, the still view. Each bug tears one more row a column sideways and stays until the bug is cleared. In motion a bugged netling also twitches a row every 3 s (use the bugs slider; reduced motion removes the twitch).

TEMPER TELL OVER TIME

The selected skin and level over 12 seconds: the picture (A high, B low, a dip for the blink), the settle or drift in columns and rows, and the brightness. Flash budget: no change closer than 200 ms.
