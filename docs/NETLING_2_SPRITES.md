## Commands

```bash
npm run proto:test                 # all prototype tests
npm run proto:audit                # 1.0 sprite audit on Iron; EGG=program|wetware|all or proto:audit:program, proto:audit:wetware
npm run proto:gallery              # writes gallery-iron.html, gallery-program.html, gallery-wetware.html (generated, gitignored)
npm run serve                      # http://localhost:5174 ; review page /prototype/netling2/ ; galleries /prototype/netling2/gallery-<egg>.html
```

- The review page (`index.html`) has a section per egg and controls that apply to every form shown: palette, tell skin, temper, care sliders (neglect), bugs, reduced motion, pose, any wearable, anchor rows. Dedicated strips exist only for neglect on Iron's four-form line and bugs on Gronk.
- The galleries are 1.0's real `gallery.html` run on one egg's forms (every wearable, state, palette, tint, prop, visitor), plus a `layers` section added by the maker (`gallery-layers.js`): every form of the egg with neglect 1 and 2, bugs 1 to 5 and two combinations (still), and the temper tell at the five levels (live; calm = reduced motion), with an optional 1.0 idle under it (a steady Iron holds it still on the beat) and the gallery's own palette, tint, scale, time and wearable controls. The gallery's renderer does not run these layers; the section draws them beside it, with the same code as the review page.
- To judge art, screenshot the pages in headless Chromium (Playwright; browsers are in `/opt/pw-browsers`) and look; draw options side by side before committing when a form is contested. The maintainer reviews from screenshots and prefers options shown together.
- **Trap:** `src/accessories.js` builds its anchor table once on first load, and `src/sim.js` imports it. Any script, test or page that draws wearables on prototype forms must import `prototype/netling2/ready.js` **first**, or the wearable code silently guesses anchors (it throws if it did not take).

## Rules the tests enforce (apply to every new or redrawn form)

- **Size.** Baby 12 wide. Teens 14 wide, 11 to 12 rows. Adults 16 wide, 13 to 15 rows. Elders 18 wide, no more than 15 rows, never shorter than the adult. Marks only `. # o + x` (main, accent/eyes, highlight, dim).
- **Frames.** Rows 0 to the neck row are identical in A and B (only the lower body animates). The body's bottom (the last row with at least 0.4 of the width painted, as the wearable code reads it) is the same row in both. A and B differ by at least 4 outline cells. Anchors (`headTop`, `eyeRow`, `mouthRow`, `neckRow`) are in range, in order, point at painted cells and are the same in A and B. Most frame bugs came from the bottom-row rule.
- **Eyes** are 2x2 groups of `o` on `eyeRow` and the row below (the generic poses find them there). Head width for eyewear comes from the `headTop` row, so that row must cover the eyes.
- **Silhouette** (1.0 audit screen, centred overlap): no same-stage pair above 0.82. The two main teens are close (0.7 to 0.82); the hidden-path teen is at least 0.1 further from each (and under 0.72) and at least 20 outline cells different. Hidden-path forms must be distinct in outline.
- **Elders.** Each role elder is closest, after scaling its adult to the elder's size, to its own adult among all nine and at least 0.75 (the unscaled score misleads for grown forms); elder pairs under 0.82. Hand-drawn, not stretched.
- **Hidden forms may break the form rules** (maintainer): Ghost's elder is 1.0's Whisper (14 wide, smaller); Blank's elder has a motion layer that changes its outline.
- **Poses.** Asleep and dead keep the awake outline; the dead X is symmetric on two-cell eyes (Wetware rounds toward the middle; Iron and Program still use 1.0's rounding, see Open).
- **Flash budget** (project rule 7): nothing flashes more than three times a second; no picture change faster than 200 ms; motion layers use 400 ms steps.

Design lessons: the street lean adds to the corp body and keeps it blocky (cutting pieces away read as broken); a hidden form's lineage can ride on marks (hood peak, shimmer, crown) not outline; a pair of marks under the mouth reads as stray pixels or extra eyes; reuse 1.0's A frame and re-time only its B frame.

### What was done in this phase (reference)

- **Care and bug effects, all 66 forms (done).** One skin for every egg, rules by stage: small forms (baby, teens) rust from the row under the eyes at 20% and 40%, adults and elders as before (12% and 30%); a form already about 40% `x` brightens cells instead; exact counts. Bugs tear toward any free side inside the frame, wrap rows with spiked edges after rows with room (so Wetware's street teen takes five), and the twitch is one column on small forms. Tests on all 66 forms; five bugs on a baby move about a quarter of its cells (worst overlap 0.74 over 20 seeds).
- **Temper pass.** `npm run proto:temper` prints the measurements; `temper-pass.test.js` holds the rules.
- **Decided (maintainer, option a): a steady Iron holds still on each beat.** 1.0's idle moves a netling almost all the time (sway and wander up to 8 columns, hover bobs a row several times a second), which hid Iron's settle (one row down for 400 ms): with the idle running, only 0 to 41% of beats had no idle movement at all from 200 ms before the settle to 600 ms after (the earlier figure, 0 to 86%, counted row changes only). Now `idleClock` in `tell.js` pauses the idle clock from 200 ms before each beat to 600 ms after (13% of the time at +1, 27% at +2) and resumes where it stopped, no jump; the renderer gives the 1.0 idle (sway, hover, wander and its hop frames) that clock instead of real time. Measured: 100% of beats clean for every idle and form height tried. Only steady Iron pauses; other eggs and levels keep real time. `idle.js` is a pure copy of the 1.0 idle rules (a test checks its constants against `src/render.js`), the review page has a "1.0 idle" selector to see it, and the real renderer does not call `idleClock` yet (nothing of 2.0 is built there). **Still open:** Iron's unsteady drift (one or two columns, smaller than the wander) and Program's strong hop (one row, 8% of slots) are still masked by the idle; they need the same kind of treatment or a decision to leave them (unsteady is the chaotic end, so a tell that is sometimes lost is less costly than at the steady end, but that is unmeasured).
- **Fixed:** a blink placed wearables on the closed-eye pose, and eyewear changes shape there (shades 17 cells to 9 on Program's baby; 66 Program form and wearable pairs), flickering the wearable on every beat. `tellPose` places them on the awake frame; the review page uses it. The review page's tell skin was a manual control and now follows the form's egg.
- **Fine as built:** no form leaves the screen at the far end of the idle with the tell added (all 66, both frames, wide elders and Blank's 24 column layer included); Wetware's skin moves nothing.
- **Clipping the tell adds** (17 form and wearable pairs, at the extremes of both the idle and the tell): Iron's Splat and Splat elder with neural jack, drone, data aura and earpiece (the drift's two columns past 1.0's own edge case); Program's corp and street teens and eight elders with the holologo (the hop's one row on top of 1.0's known 1 px holologo clip). Not changed: both only occur at the far end of the idle, and the holologo is already on the open list.
- **Decided (maintainer): during a visit the motion pauses, the look stays.** Visits are short, so no temper tell, no 1.0 idle wander and no bug twitch while one is on screen; neglect, the still bug tears and the state marks (which 1.0 also draws on a visit) stay. The sprite plays 1.0's own visit bounce (swing toward the visitor, hop on the frame; a fixed swing and no hop in calm). `visit.js` holds `visitLayers` (level 0 is the plain 1.0 rhythm, idle off, `still` for the bugs) and `visitMotion` (a copy of the render.js visit rules; a test checks the formulas against that file and that no form gets closer than the 2 columns 1.0 allows to a visitor of 12, 16 or 18 columns). The review page has a "visit (motion paused)" checkbox and the galleries a last column, "on a visit (motion paused)", asked for strongly unsteady, neglect 2 and 5 bugs: it shows the neglect and still tears with no tell, twitch or idle. Nothing calls the rule for the state marks yet.
- **Not done:** the tell with the 1.0 idle quirk in a real renderer (nothing is drawn there), Blank's layer with wearables, Metronome and idle-action art (item 4).
