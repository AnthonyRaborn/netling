WHAT THIS SHOWS

  • Top strip is read only. Everything you tap is in the bottom third.
  • Pips: ten per vital, one pip per 10 points. A wider gap marks where a state band starts. Zero or one pip is the danger band, with no label. Named states (Overclock, Overdrive) recolor the row.
  • The lane between pet and dock is always reserved. Events and wishes share it. An event closes any open tray.
  • MENU shows a dot when alerts are off. Wins reset each life, so they live in PLAY and STATUS only.
  • STYLE keeps a live preview pinned on top, shows every item with its hint (owned or locked), and has a visible scrollbar plus a "scroll for more" cue. Shell, tint and screen effect apply to the preview and the device. Sound and music only show a note here.
  • Shell recolors every border on the device. Heat pips take their color from their band (cool, warm, hot). Newly unlocked items carry a NEW tag, and the STYLE button shows a dot until each is seen.
  • Red marks each danger zone: Heat's hot end and the low end of Charge, Sync and Integrity. Overclock and Overdrive no longer recolor anything; the state word is the only mark.
  • The EGG chips switch the skin (palette and Fix names). The SCREENS chips show egg choice, flatline, compile, hibernating, a transmission and the other-tab notice. The ending is not mocked.
  • The nameplate at the top of the device holds the crest and the device label (1.0's header brand). STYLE has a CREST list (1.0's real crests and hints) and a LABEL field; the preview shows the plate. The LABEL LOCKED chip shows the locked state.
  • Music loops in the preview until stopped. With sound off (try the SOUND OFF chip) it shows a muted note and a button to turn sound on.
  • STATUS log shows 3 lines, with SHOW ALL opening a scrolling list.
  • Open: Heat reads as heat (full is bad). Decided to keep it.
